Questions

I can't find my new camera profiles

Restart Photoshop or Lightroom, open a raw photo from your camera, then open the Profile browser. They are listed as Source camera: Profile name. JPEGs are not supported.

The app says Camera Raw is not found

Install Camera Raw, then select Try again.

Some camera profiles are missing

The list comes from your Camera Raw version. Update Camera Raw to get newer ones.

Does it change Adobe's files?

No. It writes copies into its own Phan Xuan Quang folders under C:\ProgramData\Adobe\CameraRaw.

How do I uninstall it?

Remove your camera profiles on the Installed page, then delete the app folder and %LocalAppData%\CameraProfilesUnlocker.

I used version 2 or older

Old camera profiles keep working but don't show on the Installed page. Old versions are on GitHub.
